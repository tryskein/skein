# Contributing to Skein

Thanks for considering it. Read the "Licensing" section first — it affects
whether you want to contribute at all, and it would be unfair to bury it at the
bottom.

## Licensing — read this before you start

Skein is distributed under the Business Source License 1.1 (see `LICENSE`).
**Contributions are accepted on different terms**: by submitting one, you grant
us broad permission to use it, including under commercial licenses. You keep the
copyright in your own code — we get permission, not ownership. The full text is
short: [`CONTRIBUTOR-CERTIFICATE.md`](CONTRIBUTOR-CERTIFICATE.md).

Why the difference: this project sells commercial licenses. The project's
license alone would not give us the right to sublicense your contribution under
commercial terms; without that permission, we could not include your
contribution in our commercial licensing. The Certificate asks for that
permission up front, which is the honest way to do it. Our approach to licensing, in plain words, is in
the licensing FAQ: https://skein.net/licensing.

Submitting a contribution — opening a pull request, sending a patch — means you
accept the Certificate for it. There is nothing to sign.

## Security

**Do not open a public issue for a security problem.** See
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for where to send it and what to expect.
