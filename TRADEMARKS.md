# Skein Trademark Policy

The Skein source code is available under the license in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
That license covers the code, not the name: it states that it "does not grant
you any right in any trademark or logo of Licensor". This page sets out how you
may use the name "Skein" and the Skein logo (the "Marks").

The aim is simple: anyone who sees the name should be able to tell what is the
official Skein and what is not.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that your service, product or instance runs on Skein:
  "Powered by Skein", "built on Skein", "compatible with Skein".
- Refer to Skein by name in articles, talks, documentation, tutorials,
  comparisons and benchmarks.
- Use the name in an unmodified copy of Skein you distribute, or in a modified
  copy that states prominently that it has been modified and by whom.
- Offer consulting, configuration, integration or training services
  "for Skein".

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "Skein", or a confusingly similar name, as the name of your product,
  service, company or domain — for example "Skein Cloud", "SkeinHost" or
  "skein-hosting.example".
- Suggest that your service, instance or fork is official, endorsed by,
  sponsored by or affiliated with the Licensor.
- Use the logo, other than to link to the official project.
- Put the Marks next to advertising, content or services that could harm the
  reputation of Skein — for example, misleading, illegal or adult content.

## Hosting a Skein instance for others

Where the license allows you to host Skein for other people, name the service
in your own words and describe it as "Powered by Skein". Do not present it as
the official Skein service.

## Asking for permission

Write to info@sebocorp.com. Hosting providers, marketplaces and partners who want
to offer Skein under its name are welcome to ask.

This policy may be updated. The current version is the one in the official
repository.
